Before you become a UGC creator, I believe you have to understand content creation first. You need to get comfortable with creating content, showing up, and putting yourself out there.

At least, that was how it happened for me.

My journey into UGC didn't start with me deliberately searching for UGC opportunities. It started with the content I was already creating.

It all began when [Cleva](/ugc/cleva) reached out to me.

At the time, the arrangement wasn't even about creating videos for them. They simply wanted me to mention them in my posts.

I did that for a month, and after reviewing my performance, the results were actually good.

Then came the Cleva Influencer Program.

Honestly, I thought I wasn't going to be selected. I had already worked with them before, and because I wasn't getting any feedback, I started wondering if my performance had really been that good.

When cohort 2 of the program came around, Whakee tagged me in the post on X, so I applied.

And guess what? I got in!

Since joining the program, I've continued progressing. I think this is about the third time I've been retained, and we've had some results along the way.

But that wasn't the only experience that introduced me to this side of content creation.

When 2026 started, a brand also reached out to me for an influencing opportunity. It was more of a paid engagement, and I did it once before eventually stopping.

I also created an advert video for another founder voluntarily.

It was around these experiences that I started thinking more about the kind of content I was creating. I realised that what I was doing with these brand videos had a name: [UGC content creation](/ugc).

And I wanted to learn more about it.

That's how I started exploring UGC.
